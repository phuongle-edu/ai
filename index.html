About Me

Hello! I'm Phuong Birmingham. As a Learning Commons Specialist II at Tarrant County College, I operate at the intersection of student development, instructional technology, and library services. My mission is to cultivate welcoming, innovative, and highly productive environments that empower diverse student bodies to achieve academic greatness.

Within our modern Learning Commons, I guide students through academic challenges, assist in research methodologies, and optimize peer mentoring workflows. I believe that information literacy is not just about finding answers, but about building critical questioning habits that serve students well beyond their collegiate journey.
